Post to the Discord thread alongside the reply to Hdot and Hukos

**FF8 (Steam 2013) beta update — v0.6.0: the game has doors now, and it tells you what a check holds** 🔑📖

Two releases' worth. Story Keys is the headline and is **off by default**;
leave it off and this is the safe upgrade over v0.4.0: everything below went
through the live self-test and real play before tagging. The v0.4.0 vehicle
caveat still only applies if you turn Vehicle Unlocks / Vehicle Gates on.

**Story Keys (new, `story_keys`, off by default)**

FF8 finally has doors. Twenty **"Key: <area>"** items, one per world-map
entrance (Balamb, Dollet, Timber, Deling City, Winhill, Shumi Village,
Trabia Garden, Esthar City, Lunar Gate, the chocobo forests, ...). Without
the key the town's entrance simply does not work: you walk onto it, nothing
happens, and the client says `Locked: Key: Dollet`. The moment the key
lands, the door works again, right where you stand. No story faking, no
file patching: the game decides every step from a small entrance table
whether a tile leads into a town, and the client rewrites one word of that
table per missing key and puts it back when the key arrives.

- **areas** — every key is a real door and the checks inside (draw points,
  magazines, rare cards, optional bosses) need it in logic, but the story
  path is never blocked: while the story wants you inside, the door opens on
  its own.
- **story** — the doors the main line walks through stay shut too, so
  advancing the story needs keys other players hold: Fire Cavern, Galbadia
  Garden, the Tomb, Deling City, Missile Base, Trabia Garden, Edea's House,
  the Great Salt Lake, Esthar City, Lunar Gate, Sorceress Memorial, Tears'
  Point. The Balamb town and Fire Cavern keys are yours from the start so
  the opening hour never waits on another world. This is the hard gating
  Hdot asked for.
- Keys carry their `/ff8warp` destination, so Fast Travel adds nothing
  alongside them. New preset **Story Keys** = Staircase + `story_keys:
  story`. Sphere report on default options: 59 checks in sphere 1 and 10-17
  spheres; with story keys 46 and 13-16.
- What keys do not do: open a town before the story does. Early entry works
  mechanically, but a town's interior scenes assume the story state (Deling
  City's hotel lounge soft-locked in testing), so it stays off until each
  town has been surveyed.

**In-game check names, no file patching** (from v0.5.0)

The client rewrites the game's resident text in memory: an unchecked draw
point's spell shows the multiworld item it holds ("Found a draw point!
Hookshot found."), magazines and the Magical Lamp / Solomon Ring pickups are
renamed in the item menu, and the field's own "Received [X]!" lines read
"Sent [Hookshot] to Bob!". Received items still have no in-game toast; the
client and trackers show those.

**Fixes and smaller things**

- **DeathLink can no longer be dodged.** A received death sticks until your
  party actually wipes: if it lands on the killing blow the win still counts
  and it takes your next fight; Phoenix Downs on the field only delay it the
  same way. (Thanks Hdot for the sync report.)
- **Study panel with a precollected starter GF** now credits both checks
  when read, instead of leaving one until after the Fire Cavern. (Thanks
  Lexra.)
- **Sync preset** for public multiworlds: edea goal, only the check groups
  that sit on the story path, no traps, and the big lock tables off so about
  half of the 70 Disc 1 checks hold other players' items instead of your own.
- **Jukebox trap**: the music trap now runs through the game's own sound
  engine and picks from five tracks (Shuffle or Boogie, both chocobo themes,
  Laguna's silent-movie piano) until the next scene change.
- **Warp items** only exist for places in your seed (an edea seed was
  shipping Disc 2/3 warps), and the option text says plainly that warps move
  you, they never skip story.
- **Two story checks** that were missing from the beat ladder: Fisherman's
  Horizon: Garden Repaired and Lunar Base: Ragnarok Landing. 582 checks
  all-on.
- **Guide:** the Steam speed booster is recommended for multiworlds, a note
  on how long FF8 is, that story order cannot be changed, and what Story
  Keys does and does not gate.
- **Tracker:** PopTracker pack 0.12.0 shows the keys and mirrors the new
  rules. Universal Tracker needs no update.

Report anything odd with `/ff8` output and the client log. Next on the list:

compatibility matrix for people running both.
