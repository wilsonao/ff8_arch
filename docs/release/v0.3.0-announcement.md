# FF8 v0.3.0 — challenging by default

*Draft for the AP Discord thread. An update post on top of the existing beta announcement.*

---

**FF8 (Steam 2013) beta update — v0.3.0: the underdog experience is now the default** 🔒

The big feedback on the FF8 world was that it never gave you the classic Archipelago "start weak, get rebuilt by the multiworld" feeling — because in FF8 your power is **draw + junction**, and nothing was touching it. That whole system is now in the item pool, and as of v0.3.0 **it's on by default** — the out-of-the-box FF8 experience is the hardcore one:

- **Junction Locks** — Str-J, Mag-J, HP-J and the rest are items. Until "Str-J" arrives, nothing junctions to Strength and you fight on raw stats.
- **Command Locks** — Magic / GF / Draw / Item are items; until they arrive it's Attack and limit breaks. (Draw comes precollected so you're never locked out of the ~240 draw-point / draw-stat checks.)
- **GF Ability Locks** — the 49 signature abilities (refines, Enc-None, Mug, Card Mod, the stat Bonuses…) must be unlocked. You can still *learn* one — the check fires — then it's revoked until the item shows up.
- **Character Locks** — Zell/Irvine/Quistis/Rinoa/Selphie fight unjunctioned until their unlock arrives.
- **Checks-only + Progressive Magic** — drawing yields nothing; every spell's stock cap comes from the multiworld, and Fire → Fira → Firaga (plus Blizzard/Thunder/Cure/Life) arrive as progressive chains.

So a plain `game: Final Fantasy VIII` yaml now drops you into a New-Game-weak SeeD cadet and the multiworld rebuilds you piece by piece. **Want it easier?** One-click **Relaxed** preset (or just flip the options off): every check group on, all locks off, vanilla magic, generous starter kit — the full 580-check world with everything junctioned and drawing like the base game. **Core Only** gives you a quick, minimal run; **SeeD Cadet** cranks it to the max (no starter magic, one GF).

Still no file patching — it's all live-memory enforcement, so the story plays vanilla and it works alongside FFNx / Junction VIII mods. Everything is verified against the running game (automated live self-test + hands-on menu checks), and logic gates every locked check so a lock can never strand another player in your multiworld.

Grab it: <https://github.com/wilsonao/ff8_arch/releases/latest> — Launcher → **Install APWorld**. Feedback / bugs → GitHub issues on `wilsonao/ff8_arch`; please include your `logs/FF8Client_*.txt`.

> Heads-up for multiworld hosts: because FF8 now starts weak by default, its checks flow a little slower early — worth knowing if you're gating other players behind FF8 progression. The Relaxed preset restores the brisk pace.
