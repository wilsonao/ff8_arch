# FF8 v0.10.0 — Discord announcement

Draft, 2026-10-24, for posting to the Discord thread. Covers everything since v0.9.0: the reports from 3 to 23 October (BassJackal, Meyrime, DrMisunderstood, yezzdia, Revalista, Dylan) and the magic-economy rework.
Jargon is defined inline so first-time Archipelago players can follow it. Each `<!-- message N -->` block fits in one Discord message (under 2,000 characters).

**FF8 (Steam 2013) beta — v0.10.0** 🌙

Download: <https://github.com/wilsonao/ff8_arch/releases/latest> (`ff8.apworld` + PopTracker pack 0.16.0, which needs **PopTracker 0.34 or later**). Update both together. **v0.9.0 seeds keep working with the new client** and get every fix in the first two sections right away. Changes marked *new seeds* need a seed generated on v0.10.0.

Quick vocabulary: a **check** is a spot in FF8 that now sends an item to someone in the session. **Logic** is the rule set that guarantees every needed item is somewhere you can reach. The **story moment** is the game's own plot-progress counter. **Story Keys** is the optional mode where town entrances stay locked until a "Key: <town>" item arrives. **Checks-only magic** is the magic mode where drawing gives you nothing: your spells arrive as items from the multiworld. A **First Draw** check is the one for drawing a spell for the first time.

**🌙 Laguna's first dream no longer freezes your story checks** (thanks **BassJackal** for the log that showed it)
v0.9.0 added a safety net: if the story jumps several chapters at once, the client holds your story checks and your goal until you confirm. But Laguna's first dream really does rewind the story moment to the very beginning and then jump back, so the safety net fired for everyone who played the dream with the client attached. That's almost certainly why **yezzdia**'s Edea goal never completed. **Fixed**: the client recognises the dream, and a hold v0.9.0 left behind lifts itself the next time you connect. No more `/ff8keepstory` needed for this.

**🐛 Fixed (works on your current seed after updating the client)**
- **Weapon Remodel / Ultimate Weapon checks** fired as soon as a weapon showed up in the junk shop's list (reading Weapons Monthly was enough), and then the real remodel gave nothing (thanks **Meyrime**). The game marks a weapon when the shop *lists* it, not when you make it. The checks now fire when the character is actually holding the new weapon.
- **A used Magical Lamp came back** after the Diablos fight. It stays used now, and a spare Lamp an older client handed out is cleaned up.
- **First Draw for spells the multiworld sent you** never counted when you drew them yourself: the game only marks a spell "drawn" inside battle. The client now spots the draw itself, from a draw point or in battle (thanks **DrMisunderstood**, whose Key: Sorceress Memorial sat behind First Draw: Flare).
- **An enemy casting a spell no longer counts as you drawing it** (an Elite Soldier's Aura sent First Draw: Aura).
- **Key: Lunar Gate and Key: Sorceress Memorial opened each other's door**, ever since Story Keys arrived in v0.6.0. Fixed, and the client corrects an older seed's doors when you connect.
- **Centra Ruins**: a battle fought on the ruins' world-map tiles could drop you inside without the key. Locked doors now re-lock instantly after a fight.

**🐛 More fixes**
- **`/ff8warp esthar`** on Disc 3 put you inside the city model. It now lands you on foot outside, where the Ragnarok sets down. After any warp, a ship you own parks beside you after your next battle.
- **The Ragnarok item** now says in the log when it does nothing: Vehicle Unlocks is off, or the story already gave you the ship.
- **Spell names in the menus**: renamed draw points (they show the item you'll find there) also renamed the spell in the main menu and in battle, so Esuna read "Giant's Tonic". Menus and battle show the real spell again, and descriptions no longer get cut off at a dangling "for".
- **A GF ability item for an ability you had already learned** gives it back straight away.
- **Closing the game** while the client was attached left it saying "Attached" forever. It notices now.
- **Tutorial screens** should no longer trigger a false "Another save was loaded".
- **`/ff8`** now shows the difficulty settings your seed was made with: AP Multiplier, Enemy Power and Triple Triad rules. The AP bonus is added *after* each battle, so the results screen still shows the normal amount.
- **Warp destinations** the story hasn't reached yet say so, and are announced again when they open.
- **Archipelago 0.6.8** is supported (0.6.7 still works), and switching rooms or slots in one client session now starts clean.

**🔮 Magic economy rework** (*new seeds*, checks-only magic)
Spells came in far too slowly with ×5–×20 packs. Now:
- **"<Spell> Stock" items** exist for every spell except Apocalypse, including **Scan** and the spells that Elemental and Status Defense junctions need, which had no source before.
- Each copy's size is rolled when the seed is generated, so the seed's magic adds up to a share of what your party can hold (6 characters × 32 spells × 100 = 19,200).
- **New option: Magic Abundance** (25–300%, default **150%**): how much magic the multiworld sends over a whole game. On the default, a full-game seed carries around 230 magic items, about 28,800 spells, 60 to 195 at a time. Shorter goals send proportionally less.
- **Less predictable strength**: about a third of spells now arrive one tier early or late. A lucky Firaga in the first hour is on purpose.

**🔒 Logic fixes** (*new seeds*)
- **The Queen of Cards' cards** need the key to every place she can be standing (seven of them). Kiros and Phoenix were only checking one.
- **Draw points moved to where they really are**:
  - The Galbadia Garden Auditorium Double is a Disc 1 draw point, so Edea-goal seeds have it now.
  - The four "Centra Excavation Site" points (Holy, Silence, Ultima, Confuse) are inside **Lunatic Pandora** on Disc 3; logic had them two discs early.
  - Laguna's second dream (Sleep, Confuse) is a one-time visit, so those never hold anything you need.
  - The world-map point above the Fire Cavern is fixed too.
- **Enemies Scanned** tiers are now 5 / 10 / **15** / **25** (were 20 and 30).
- **New check: Siren Learns ST Med-RF**, her one refine ability that wasn't a check. With GF ability locks on it has its own unlock item, so "GF Abilities Learned: 140" becomes **135**.

**🖥️ New: the FF8 tab in the client window**
The client now opens on an **FF8** tab where every command is a button:
- game and server status at a glance, plus what's still locked
- the Battle Assist and DeathLink switches (**DeathLink**: when one player dies, everyone in the session who has it on dies too)
- one button per warp destination you've unlocked
- the look-ups: status, unsent checks, magic ledger, and so on

A click runs exactly the command you could type, and the result appears in the log beside the buttons. Anything that can't be undone asks first. The command prompt at the bottom still works from every tab.

**🗺️ Tracker 0.16.0** (PopTracker 0.34+)
- **Precise maps**: the world and area maps are drawn from FF8's own world data, with one pin per landmark at its exact spot. Every town has an overhead floor plan with each draw point and check where it really is.
- **Follows you into towns**: walk into Balamb and the tracker opens Balamb's floor plan; leave and it goes back to the area map (needs the v0.10.0 client).
- The chapter shown now follows your story moment, instead of waiting for a story check.

**📖 New: an FAQ** (<https://github.com/wilsonao/ff8_arch/blob/main/docs/faq.md>) explains every option in full and answers the questions asked most in this thread.

**Heads-up**: most of this release was checked against the game's own code and our automated tests, but a few fixes (the Centra Ruins re-lock, the tutorial message, the ship following a warp) haven't had a full playthrough yet. If anything looks off, post your client log here.

**Still on the list**: the tracker reading your goal (an Edea seed still shows Disc 2+ areas), a switch to turn off Potion warps, a per-character magic cap, enemy strength above 100%, and more sidequest checks (Battle Meter, D-District crates, the Master Fisherman, the Grease Monkey).

Thanks again, everyone. Most of this release came straight from your reports. 💙
