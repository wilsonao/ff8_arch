# FF8 v0.8.0 — Discord announcement (draft)

Post to the Discord thread once v0.8.0 is tagged and the release assets are
up. Every fix below came from reports in this thread between 23 and 27 Sep.
Jargon is defined inline so first-time Archipelago players can follow it.

---

**FF8 (Steam 2013) beta update — v0.8.0: the "thank you for the bug reports" release** 🛠️

Download: <https://github.com/wilsonao/ff8_arch/releases/latest> (`ff8.apworld` + the PopTracker pack, pack 0.14.0). Update the client and the pack together. Seeds generated on v0.7.0 keep working with the new client, but the four "Steps Taken" checks were renamed, so a v0.7.0 seed's tracker will show those four under their old names.

Quick vocabulary for anyone new: a **check** is a place in FF8 where you would normally get something and which now sends an item to someone in the session; **logic** is the rule set that keeps every needed item somewhere you can actually reach; a **sphere** is one step of that; the **story moment** is the game's own progression counter, which the client reads to know where you are in the plot.

**Save safety (please read if you use Vehicle Unlocks)**

- With Vehicle Unlocks on, the client briefly sets the story moment to a Disc 3 value (3167) during world-map battles so the Ragnarok can spawn early. If the client crashed, lost the game, or disconnected at exactly that moment, the value could stay behind and end up in a save, and your game would think it was on Disc 3. One player had to fix a save by hand. **Fixed**: every error path now restores the real value first, the client remembers what it was faking, and a save that did capture the value is repaired automatically the next time it is loaded with the client running. `/ff8moment` is the manual fallback.
- The Ragnarok is no longer spawned around battles fought from inside a town or dungeon (only world-map fights), and it stops re-parking itself on top of you after every fight if it is already nearby.

**Checks that fired when they should not have**

- Starting a new seed in the same client after finishing another could credit the old seed's checks from the save still sitting in memory on the title screen. Fixed: saves are now stamped per seed, the title screen is never read, and the client forgets the previous seed's state when it connects.
- **"First Draw: Triple"** and friends fired when the spell was *received* from another player. The game marks a spell "obtained" the first time it notices stock, and the client's deliveries skipped that. Fixed: a spell you were sent only counts once you really draw it.
- **"Steps Taken: 20,000"** fired on the first world-map visit. The world map counts about thirty steps a second, so the tiers are now 100k / 200k / 270k / 400k, matched to where real saves reach them.
- "Battle won: encounter 514" repeating during a Triple Triad game: fixed, card games are no longer mistaken for battles (this also stops them arming DeathLink).

**Logic and soft-locks**

- **Deling City locked behind you** (Story Keys, story mode): the train takes you into Deling without its key, and leaving for the Tomb shut the door. Now, in both key modes, a town's door is open by itself while the story is walking through it; the key is for visiting outside that window. Same fix covers the Timber forest road where Laguna's second dream plays.
- **The Islands Closest to Heaven and Hell** are no longer in logic on Disc 1 just because you own the Ragnarok. Their level-100 monsters need a Disc 3 party, so the ship opens them from the Esthar chapter on. No more Deling City key on the Island Closest to Hell.
- **Warping to Esthar on Disc 1** (or any town before its chapter) landed on an empty map or soft-locked (Fisherman's Horizon). Warps now wait for the story to reach the town, except Winhill, Shumi Village and the Centra Ruins, which are safe early.
- Draw points moved to where they really are: the Balamb Garden **Cafeteria Demi** point belongs to the Garden Revolt (it does not exist during the prologue — thanks Gaothaire), the **Dollet Silence** point moved to the Dollet revisit, **Shenand Hill** waits for the mobile Garden, and the **Fire draw point mid-bridge near Timber** is now a check (it was missing from the table).
- Blue Magic: **Electrocute** now expects Deling City (Creeps in the sewers), **Fire Breath** and **Ray-Bomb** moved to the Disc 3 chapters where their items actually drop.
- **Gil Snatch** never takes you below 3000 gil, so the Timber train is always affordable.
- Getting **Gilgamesh** before **Odin** no longer cancels Odin for the rest of the game.

**In-game text**

- A draw point's spell rename ("Meltdown x5" where Cure was) no longer follows you into battle and into the enemy Draw list.
- A magazine found at a renamed spot gets its own name back once its check is sent (it always kept working; only the name was stuck).

**Also in this release** (from 8910bbc, unreleased until now): the `/ff8assist enc` log spam with Character Locks is gone, and the in-game renames work over Maelstrom's shuffled draw points and magic table.

**Not in this release yet**, but on the list from your feedback: an AP multiplier, an enemy level cap, and a switch to turn the Random rule off in Triple Triad. Keep the reports coming — this whole release is you.
