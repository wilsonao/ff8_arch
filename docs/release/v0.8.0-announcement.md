# FF8 v0.8.0 — "The DrMisunderstood Release" — Discord announcement

Released 2026-09-30. Final text as handed over for posting to the Discord thread. Every fix below came from reports in this thread between 23 and 27 Sep.
Jargon is defined inline so first-time Archipelago players can follow it.

---

**FF8 (Steam 2013) beta — v0.8.0, "The DrMisunderstood Release"** 🛠️

Download: <https://github.com/wilsonao/ff8_arch/releases/latest> (`ff8.apworld` + the PopTracker pack 0.14.0). Update both together. Seeds made on v0.7.0 still work with the new client; only the four "Steps Taken" checks were renamed, so on an old seed the tracker shows those under their old names.

This one is named for **DrMisunderstood**, who spent a weekend breaking the game in every direction and wrote it all down: two full restarts, a Disc-1 Ragnarok tour, a soft-lock or two, and a dozen "hmm, that's odd" posts that each turned out to be a real bug. Nearly everything below traces back to that thread. Thanks also to **Gaothaire** (the Cafeteria draw point), **AmphaRaven** (the corrupted save), **Konzera** (First Draw and the magazine names), **Loto** (the option requests) and **Hukos** (confirming the invisible-Squall thing is vanilla).

Quick vocabulary for anyone new: a **check** is a place in FF8 where you would normally get something and which now sends an item to someone in the session. **Logic** is the rule set that keeps every needed item somewhere you can actually reach. The **story moment** is the game's own progress counter, which the client reads to know where you are in the plot. **Story Keys** is the optional mode where town entrances are locked until a "Key: <town>" item arrives.

**🩹 Save safety (read this if you use Vehicle Unlocks)**
- With Vehicle Unlocks on, the client briefly sets the story moment to a Disc 3 value during world-map battles so the Ragnarok can spawn early. If the client crashed or disconnected at exactly that moment, the value could stay behind and get saved, and your game would think it was on Disc 3. AmphaRaven hit this and had to repair a save by hand. **Fixed**: every error path restores the real value first, the client remembers what it was faking, and a save that already captured the value is repaired automatically the next time it is loaded with the client running. `/ff8moment` is the manual fallback.
- The Ragnarok only spawns around world-map fights now (not fights inside towns and dungeons), and it stops re-parking itself on top of you after every battle when it is already nearby.

**🐛 Checks that fired when they should not have**
- Starting a new seed in the same client could credit the *old* seed's checks from the save still sitting in memory on the title screen. Saves are now stamped per seed, the title screen is never read, and the client forgets the previous seed's state on connect.
- **"First Draw: Triple"** and friends fired when the spell was *received* from another player. A spell you were sent now only counts once you really draw it.
- **"Steps Taken: 20,000"** fired on the first world-map visit. The world map counts about thirty steps a second, so the tiers are now 100k / 200k / 270k / 400k, matched against real saves.
- "Battle won: encounter 514" spamming during a Triple Triad game: card games are no longer mistaken for battles (which also means they no longer arm DeathLink).

**🔒 Soft-locks and logic**
- **Deling City locked behind you** (Story Keys): the train takes you into Deling without its key, and leaving for the Tomb shut the door. Now a town's door opens by itself while the story is walking through it, in both key modes. The key is for visiting *outside* that window. Same fix covers the Timber forest road where Laguna's second dream plays.
- **The Islands Closest to Heaven and Hell** are out of Disc-1 logic. Owning the Ragnarok no longer puts 61 level-100-monster draw points in reach on Disc 1, so no more Deling City key waiting on the Island Closest to Hell.
- **Warping to Esthar on Disc 1** landed on an empty map, and Fisherman's Horizon soft-locked. Warps now wait for the story to reach the town, except Winhill, Shumi Village and the Centra Ruins, which are safe early.
- Draw points moved to where they really are: the Balamb Garden **Cafeteria Demi** point belongs to the Garden Revolt, **Dollet Silence** to the Dollet revisit, **Shenand Hill** waits for the mobile Garden, and the **Fire draw point mid-bridge near Timber** is now a check (it was missing from the table).
- Blue Magic: **Electrocute** expects Deling City (Creeps in the sewers), **Fire Breath** and **Ray-Bomb** moved to the Disc 3 chapters where their items actually drop.
- Getting **Gilgamesh** before **Odin** no longer cancels Odin for the rest of the game.

**📝 In-game text**
- A draw point's spell rename ("Meltdown x5" where Cure was) no longer follows you into battle and into the enemy Draw list.
- A magazine found at a renamed spot gets its own name back once its check is sent. It always kept working as the magazine; only the name was stuck.

**📖 Answers to the questions that came up**, now in the player guide under "What exactly triggers a check": limit breaks count when *learned*, not used; magazines keep working under their multiworld name; Enemy Scanned counts Scan pages viewed; Odin and Gilgamesh are independent items; `/ff8` shows which characters, junctions and commands you currently have unlocked (yes, Zell's junctions can be in your starting inventory); and Stat Ladder checks are the game's own save-file counters, nothing to do with Steam achievements.

**Also in this release**: the `/ff8assist enc` log spam with Character Locks is gone, and in-game renames work over Maelstrom's shuffled draw points and magic table.

**Not in yet, but on the list from your feedback**: an AP multiplier, an enemy level cap, and a switch to turn off the Random rule in Triple Triad. Two things I still need to test live before I can fix them: using the Magical Lamp when you already own Diablos, and Lunatic Pandora showing up alongside the early Ragnarok. And the invisible-Squall-with-Ragnarok during the SeeD-uniform stretch is a vanilla bug, so that one stays.

Keep the reports coming. This whole release is you.
