**FF8 (Steam 2013) beta update — v0.5.0: the game tells you what a check holds** 📖

Stability: this release does not touch the world map, so the v0.4.0 vehicle
caveat still only applies if you turn Vehicle Unlocks / Vehicle Gates on
(both off by default). Everything below went through the live self-test and
real battles before tagging.

What changed:

- **In-game check names, no file patching.** The client rewrites the
  game's resident text in memory: an unchecked draw point's spell shows the
  multiworld item it holds ("Found a draw point! Hookshot found."), magazines
  and the Magical Lamp / Solomon Ring pickups are renamed in the item menu,
  and the field's own "Received [X]!" lines read "Sent [Hookshot] to Bob!".
  Received items still have no in-game toast; the client and trackers show
  those.
- **DeathLink can no longer be dodged.** A received death sticks until your
  party actually wipes: if it lands on the killing blow the win still counts
  and it takes your next fight; Phoenix Downs on the field only delay it the
  same way. (Thanks Hdot for the sync report.)
- **Sync preset** for public multiworlds: edea goal, only the check groups
  that sit on the story path, no traps, and the big lock tables off so about
  half of the 70 Disc 1 checks hold other players' items instead of your own.
- **Warp items** now only exist for places in your seed (an edea seed was
  shipping Disc 2/3 warps), and the option text says plainly that warps move
  you, they never skip story.
- **Two new story checks** that were missing from the beat ladder:
  Fisherman's Horizon: Garden Repaired and Lunar Base: Ragnarok Landing.
  582 checks all-on.
- **Guide:** the Steam speed booster is recommended for multiworlds, a note
  on how long FF8 is, and that story order cannot be changed.
- **Tracker:** PopTracker pack 0.11.0 carries the two new checks. Universal
  Tracker needs no update.

Next up is Story Keys: key items that physically lock world-map entrances,
with the story-required ones as hard gates. Details in the reply below.
