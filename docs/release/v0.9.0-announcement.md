# FF8 v0.9.0 — Discord announcement

Draft, 2026-10-02, for posting to the Discord thread. Covers the reports from 28 Sep to 1 Oct (DrMisunderstood, yezzdia, TimShuggah) plus the options requested earlier.
Jargon is defined inline so first-time Archipelago players can follow it.

---

**FF8 (Steam 2013) beta — v0.9.0** 🛩️

Download: <https://github.com/wilsonao/ff8_arch/releases/latest> (`ff8.apworld` + PopTracker pack 0.15.0). Update both together. **Seeds made on v0.8.0 keep working with the new client**, and most fixes below apply to them right away. The logic fixes (marked *new seeds*) only apply to seeds generated on v0.9.0.

Quick vocabulary: a **check** is a spot in FF8 that now sends an item to someone in the session. **Logic** is the rule set that guarantees every needed item is somewhere you can reach. The **story moment** is the game's own plot-progress counter. **Story Keys** is the optional mode where town entrances stay locked until a "Key: <town>" item arrives. **Vehicle Unlocks** is the option that lets the Ragnarok arrive as an item, long before the story would give it to you.

**🛩️ The early Ragnarok no longer breaks the story** (thanks **DrMisunderstood** for the Disc-1 world tour)
- With the early Ragnarok you could fly into Timber before Dollet, Edea's House on Disc 1, Fisherman's Horizon, the Great Salt Lake and so on, and get later-disc scenes that soft-locked or ended the game. The cause was ours. To make the ship appear, the client briefly tells the game it's Disc 3. The world map keeps its own copy of that value for deciding which towns let you in, and that copy stayed at "Disc 3" after every fight. **Fixed**: the client puts the real value back as soon as the ship has appeared. Towns now open only when your story reaches them. Tested live: the ship still spawns, boards, flies and lands, Balamb Garden lets you in, and Deling City correctly refuses a Disc-1 visit.
- **Safety net**: if a scene ever pushes the story ahead several chapters in one step, the client now **holds** those story checks (and your goal) instead of sending them all, and tells you. Reload a save from before it and everything is normal. Or type `/ff8keepstory` to keep playing from there. Loading a later save or catching up after playing offline never trips this.
- The **Edea goal** can't be completed by a later-disc scene any more (Edea's House, the Fisherman's Horizon welcome and the Dobe talk all did it).

**🐛 Fixed (works on your current seed after updating the client)**
- **"Death x5" gave you Pain, and "Pain x5" gave Float.** Wrong spell numbers; both now give what they say.
- **Status-Attack-Junction "revoked" right after you junction it.** Two junction slots were swapped inside the client, so locking Elemental Defense wiped your status-attack junction. The client now also says *which* junction it removed and which item unlocks it.
- **Drawing with Squall out of the party took the magic off Squall** (checks-only magic mode). The excess now comes back from whoever drew it.
- **"You need a key for the Chocobo Forests" at Trabia Garden.** The two share a world-map square; the message now names the right door.
- **Card Club**: the checks for Dr. Kadowaki, the King and Joker fired during their dialogue. They now fire when you **win a card game** against them.
- **Monsters Felled** checks arrived late: the game only updates its total when you enter a town. They now count world-map kills right away.
- **The Magical Lamp works when you already own Diablos** from the multiworld. The client sets him aside while your menu is open so the game lets you use the Lamp, then gives him back.
- **Fast travel by Potion**:
  - The destination labels were wrong, and Seifer and Edea were listed even though you can't pick them. The game counts the characters who have joined so far, in roster order. The labels now follow that, and the client tells you which destinations are `/ff8warp`-only.
  - A Potion drunk in battle, used in a town's menu or sold no longer warps you or gets refunded. Only a Potion used from the world-map menu warps.
- **Loading a different save** (Ctrl+R) could warp you, hand out Potions, or credit the old save's progress. The client now notices every load and starts fresh.
- **Junction VIII** (thanks **TimShuggah**): if checks don't send when launching through Junction VIII, the client now says when the game is running as administrator, or when it found the 2000 PC release's `FF8.exe` instead of the Steam one. It also names the exact game file it attached to.

**🔒 Logic fixes (new seeds)** — the Disc 3 dead end DrMisunderstood hit
- **GF ability chains**: some abilities only appear after another is learned (Leviathan's GFRecov Med-RF needs Supt Mag-RF first, Quezacotl's Card Mod needs Card). With the lock options on, logic now waits for the unlock item of the earlier ability. In DrMisunderstood's seed, Key: Lunar Gate sat behind exactly that chain. "GF Abilities Learned: 150" becomes **140** for the same reason.
- **Tonberries Culled** checks now need Key: Centra Ruins (Tonberries only live there).
- **The Bika Snowfield peninsula Flare (#2)** needs the Ragnarok; the Garden can't reach it. It held a Great Salt Lake key last time.
- **No Story Keys in Ultimecia's Castle**: there's no world map left to use them on.

**⚙️ New options** (all off by default)
- **Enemy Power** (25–100%): enemies fight at a percentage of their strength. Useful with the junction and ability locks, where your party lags behind FF8's level scaling. *Experimental.*
- **AP Multiplier** (1–4x): your GFs earn more AP per battle. *Experimental.*
- **Triple Triad Rules**: turn the Random rule off everywhere (or force Open as well), so nobody has to learn the Dollet-hotel abolishing trick 😅.
- **Triple Triad Rule Checks**: Random-abolition and region-win checks have their own on/off switch now.
- **Gil Snatch** traps can take everything you have now. They're meant to be goofy; you might be walking to Timber.

**📊 Tracker**: every junction and command lock has its own icon next to the counters, so you can see which ones are open (DrMisunderstood had to build his own tracker for this; now it's built in).

**📖 Answers to questions from the thread**
- *yezzdia — Edea goal plus an early Ragnarok?* Everything past Disc 1 is vanilla in an Edea seed: those places aren't checks, so draws, Jumbo Cactuar and so on behave like the normal game. Lock options still apply everywhere.
- *story_gates "pre-0.4 behaviour"?* That text is rewritten. In short, story_gates never blocks you and adds no checks. You always play the whole story in order; it only decides how many items you're expected to have before each chapter. With `off`, the only requirement is the GF count for Disc 3.
- *Timber Maniacs from the Centra Ruins with no magazine in sight?* That's the vanilla game: it hands you that issue silently the first time you arrive.

**Still on the list**: a "free roam" switch for the early Ragnarok, more fast-travel destinations than characters, and new sidequest checks (the Garden departments during the NORG fight, the Grease Monkey, the Master Fisherman, Shumi Village, the Winhill vases).

Thanks again, everyone. Most of this release came straight from your reports.
