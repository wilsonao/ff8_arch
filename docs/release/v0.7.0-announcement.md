# FF8 v0.7.0 — Discord announcement (draft)

Post to the Discord thread. v0.6.0 was never announced, so its headline
feature (Story Keys) is folded in; `v0.6.0-announcement.md` is superseded.
Jargon is defined inline so first-time Archipelago players can follow it.

---

**FF8 (Steam 2013) beta update — v0.7.0: doors, early entry, and a Battle Assist menu** 🔑🚀⚔️

Download: <https://github.com/wilsonao/ff8_arch/releases/latest> (`ff8.apworld` + the PopTracker pack). Everything new is **off by default**, so updating is safe: leave the new options alone and the game plays exactly as it did on v0.5.0.

A few words first, for anyone new to this. In Archipelago every game's rewards are shuffled into one shared pool across all the players in the session. Each place in FF8 where you would normally get something (a GF, a draw point, a magazine, a story beat) is a **check**: reaching it sends whatever item was hidden there to whoever owns it, and your own rewards arrive from other players' games. **Logic** is the set of rules the generator follows so that nothing you need is hidden somewhere you cannot reach yet. A **sphere** is one step of that: sphere 1 is everything you can do from the very start, sphere 2 what opens once sphere 1's items are in, and so on. A **preset** is a ready-made bundle of settings on the options page.

**Story Keys (from v0.6.0, `story_keys`, off by default)**

FF8 has doors now. Twenty **"Key: <area>"** items, one per world-map entrance (Balamb, Dollet, Timber, Deling City, Winhill, Shumi Village, Trabia Garden, Esthar City, Lunar Gate, the chocobo forests, ...). Until the key reaches you, that town's entrance simply does not work: you walk onto it, nothing happens, and the client says `Locked: Key: Dollet`. The moment the key lands, the door works again, right where you stand. Nothing is patched and the story is never faked; the game decides every step, from a small table, whether a tile leads into a town, and the client rewrites one word of that table per missing key.

- **areas** mode: every key is a real door and the checks inside it need the key in logic, but the story path is never blocked. When the story wants you inside a town, that door opens on its own.
- **story** mode: the doors the main story walks through stay shut too, so advancing the plot needs keys that other players are holding. This is the real multiworld gating people asked for. Balamb town and the Fire Cavern keys are yours from the start so the opening hour never waits on another world.
- Keys carry their `/ff8warp` destination (the client command that teleports you to a place on the world map), so the separate Warp items are gone. New preset **Story Keys**.

**Early entry (new, needs `vehicle_unlocks`)**

With Vehicle Unlocks on, the Ragnarok airship is an item that can arrive at any point, and you fly it long before the story hands it over. Until now that only helped with world-map draw points. Now five places open early too, ship in hand: the **Tomb of the Unknown King**, the **Centra Ruins**, the **chocobo forests**, **Winhill** and **Shumi Village**. Their draw points, magazines, the Brothers, the Chicobo card and the first chocobo forest join logic from the start (with Story Keys on, you also need that area's key). Winhill and Shumi were walked room by room, villager by villager, on a Disc 1 save, including saving and reloading inside, before being switched on. Other towns stay closed before the story reaches them: Deling City's hotel lounge soft-locks if you get there early, and a town only opens once it has been surveyed the same way.

**Battle Assist (new, client-side, `/ff8assist`)**

The 2013 PC version has none of the Remastered boosters, so the client provides its own. All of these live only in the client, are off by default, and change nothing about your options, logic, or save:

- `/ff8assist oneshot` — **One Shot mode.** Every enemy in a random encounter is left with 1 HP the moment the fight starts, so the first hit ends it and the game hands out EXP, AP and drops as normal. Bosses and scripted fights are always fought for real.
- `/ff8assist enc` — **no random encounters at all.** The client keeps Enc-None equipped on your party (no Diablos needed), from the first step of the game if you like, and takes it back out when you turn it off. Handy when you just want to get somewhere.
- `/ff8assist atb` — ATB bars always full. `/ff8assist hp` — HP kept topped up (nobody is revived).
- `/ff8assist on` turns everything on, `/ff8assist off` everything off. Turn `oneshot` and `enc` off before fights you want to Draw or Card in, or for the Tonberry King.
- DeathLink (the option where one player's death kills everyone) always wins: a received death cannot be dodged by a one-hit fight, and `enc` is lifted until the death has found a fight to land in.

**In-game check names, no file patching** (from v0.5.0)

An unchecked draw point's spell now reads as the multiworld item it holds ("Found a draw point! Hookshot found."), magazines and the Magical Lamp / Solomon Ring pickups are renamed in the item menu, and the field's own "Received [X]!" lines read "Sent [Hookshot] to Bob!". The real Magical Lamp and Solomon Ring keep their own names once they arrive. Received items still have no in-game toast; the client and trackers show those.

**Fixes and smaller things**

- **DeathLink can no longer be dodged.** A received death sticks until your party actually wipes: if it lands on the killing blow the win still counts and it takes your next fight, and Phoenix Downs on the field only delay it the same way. (Thanks Hdot for the sync report.)
- **Study panel with a precollected starter GF** now credits both checks when read. (Thanks Lexra.)
- **Sync preset** for public multiworlds: the short `edea` goal (the game ends at the Deling City parade), only the check groups that sit on the story path, no traps, and the big lock tables off so about half of the Disc 1 checks hold other players' items instead of your own.
- **Jukebox trap** runs through the game's own sound engine and picks from five tracks until the next scene change.
- **Warp items** only exist for places in your seed, and the option text says plainly that warps move you and never skip story.
- **Two story checks** that were missing: Fisherman's Horizon: Garden Repaired and Lunar Base: Ragnarok Landing. 582 checks with everything on.
- **Shumi warp** now lands beside the village dome instead of out in the snow.
- **Guide:** the Steam speed booster is recommended for multiworlds, a note on how long FF8 is, and what Story Keys does and does not gate.
- **Tracker:** PopTracker pack 0.13.0 shows the keys and the early-entry areas and mirrors all the new rules. Universal Tracker needs no update.

**Stability note:** Story Keys, Vehicle Unlocks and early entry are experimental and off by default. If you turn them on and something looks wrong, post the output of `/ff8` and the client log. Next on the list: a compatibility matrix for people running the Maelstrom randomizer alongside this.
